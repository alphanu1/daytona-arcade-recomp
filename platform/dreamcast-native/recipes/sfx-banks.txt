# The sound effect banks of build_sfx.sh: which rendered flows are resident together, and their sample coding.
#   BANK CODING KEY...   KEY: a key of render.tsv (tools/prepare_sfx.cpp), or a hex range FIRST-LAST of keys with
#                        the same prefix (9e01-9e2d). CODING: pcm8 (afx_vgm's default) or adpcm (afx_vgm --adpcm).
# Every bank BANK becomes sfx/BANK.afb + sfx/BANK.dsx; code/audio/sfx.c loads sfx/race.* at start-up, before any music.
#
# race: everything a race can play, resident next to the race music. AICA budget (build_sfx.sh checks it): the
# arena AICAflow reports (2,072,768 bytes) less the largest recording the native race plays (03 The King of Speed,
# 1,561,766 bytes with its flow) leaves about 511 KB.
#   e0..e9     the ten engine layers (rpm x6, opponent left/right, slip, skid): true loops, PCM8
#   9e01-9e2d  channel 14's effect kit: crashes, wall rub, passes, skid and rev, air drill, tool drop, coin, signal
#   ae2109-c   the start voices THREE, TWO, ONE, GO
#   ae2010     the crowd at every lap (9e11's sample: no bytes of its own), ae2002/ae2003 the slot machine
#
# voices: the announcer's phrases a race can play beyond the countdown (race_intent_data.h's rows and specials, the
# lap call ae2101, the record ae2124, the finish calls ae2136..ae2138, ae2100): 50, 588 KB of ADPCM samples. They do
# not fit beside the PCM8 race music: build_sfx.sh --voices stream (default) writes sfx/voices.pak for the streamed
# slot (NATIVE_SFX_VOICE_STREAM=1); --voices resident adds them to the race bank (beside the race music in ADPCM).
race pcm8 e0-e9
race adpcm 9e01-9e2d
race adpcm ae2109-ae210c ae2010 ae2002 ae2003
voices adpcm ae2100 ae2101 ae2105-ae2108 ae210f ae2111 ae2114-ae2117 ae211a ae2120-ae2131 ae2133-ae2138 ae2163-ae216d ae2170 ae2171
